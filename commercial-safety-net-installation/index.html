commercial construction

fall arrest nets that stay out of the way

Commercial safety netting protects the work area below while leaving the space above clear for the job. Each installation is considered against the structure, anchor points, fall distance, clearance and access available on site.

For larger or staged work, send the plans, programme and site details. We’ll confirm the areas that can be netted, the installation sequence, expected hire duration and when removal needs to happen.

what the service covers

  • 01Planning around access, anchorage, clearance and timing.
  • 02FASET-certified safety net installation.
  • 03Safety net hire for the agreed build period.
  • 04Installation checks and handover information.
  • 05Planned removal when the protected work is complete.
