the complete service

from first check to final removal.

Send the location, approximate net area, build type and timing. We’ll confirm whether the site suits a safety net system and line the work up with your programme.

  1. 01Plan. Check the work area, access, anchor points, fall distance, clearance and timing.
  2. 02Install. Inspect the nets and complete the FASET-certified installation.
  3. 03Hire. Leave the nets in place for the agreed build period.
  4. 04Handover. Provide installation checks and details for the site file.
  5. 05Remove. Plan removal once the protected work is finished.
